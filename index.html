The case

What works, what to weigh, who it suits.

Each point cuts both ways. Neither side is a verdict.

StrengthsWhy buyers are looking

  • A station-side estate.Lorong Chuan MRT is within about 400 m, with the Bishan and Serangoon interchanges one stop away.Reported
  • Scale, and choice.More than 1,000 homes on two sites should mean a wide choice of layouts and stacks. The unit mix has not been released.Confirmed
  • An established neighbourhood.Malls such as NTP+, Junction 8 and NEX, and several schools, are close by (see Location).Reported
  • A price benchmark nearby.Chuan Park has 900 recorded new sales, so buyers can see what a new launch here has actually sold for.Confirmed

ConsiderationsBefore you commit

  • A clear premium over resale.Chuan Park's new sales have a median of $2,684 psf over the last 12 months, about 28–45% above nearby 99-year resales. Compare at the same total budget, not just psf.Confirmed
  • A very large build.Over 1,000 homes means a long construction period, and many similar homes for sale or rent once it completes.
  • The details that decide value are unknown.Prices, unit mix, stack facing, maintenance fees and the completion date are not published yet.
  • A change for the city.Lorong Chuan is on the Circle Line only. Trips to the city usually mean changing at Bishan or Serangoon, one stop away.Reported

Land price per square foot per plot ratio (psf ppr) is what the developers paid for each square foot of floor area they may build. The two sites cost $1,376 and $1,331 psf ppr, $1,355 combined. Selling prices also cover construction, financing, marketing and profit, so land cost is a starting point, not a formula.

The 75% loan is an illustration: your actual loan depends on any other home loans, your age and the tenure.

How this is worked out
  • Bank loan: 75% applies if you have no other home loan, and the loan runs up to 30 years and ends by age 65. With one home loan still outstanding, it is 45%, with at least 25% in cash. Groundwork works out your own limit.
  • Downpayment: at least 5% must be cash. The other 20% can be cash or CPF Ordinary Account savings, if you are eligible to use them.
  • Instalment: on the full loan at the rate you enter. 4% is a planning rate, not a bank package. You pay less during construction, as the loan is drawn in stages. Banks usually count the tenure from the first drawdown, so the instalment after completion can be higher than shown; Groundwork shows this.
  • Stamp duty: Buyer's Stamp Duty at IRAS rates. ABSD is not included: if you buy before selling, you may need to pay it upfront, and eligible buyers may claim a refund if they sell within six months of the new home's TOP or CSC, whichever is earlier.
